Conclusion: every WarpTransformer number currently in the paper was produced with
velocity.var(), for both pretraining and calibration.

The two losses are effectively equivalent
  Because make_monotone_warp normalises the warp so that w(T-1) = T-1, the mean velocity is
  pinned to v_bar = 1 - delta_0 / sum(delta), and the following identity holds exactly:

      mean((v - 1)^2) = ((T - 2) / (T - 1)) * var_unbiased(v) + (delta_0 / sum(delta))^2

  With T = 200 the scale factor is 198/199 = 0.995. The extra term is about (1/T)^2 = 2.5e-5
  near identity and only penalises w(0) drifting away from 0. Verified numerically on 1,000
  random monotone warps at four raw-logit scales: ratio (v-1)^2 / var = 0.9950-0.9952, and the
  identity matches to 5e-7.

Status of the original checkpoint
  The original variance-trained checkpoint no longer exists (it was overwritten on 2026-10-09).
  The replacement, checkpoints/warp_transformer_baseline.pt, was retrained with the current
  (v - 1)^2 loss, the same architecture, seed and 10,000 iterations. All new numbers produced
  for the paper (significance tests, seeded calibration, ablation, held-out anchors, anchor
  count) come from this checkpoint with the (v - 1)^2 loss in calibration, so the paper's
  (v - 1)^2 equation is exactly correct for them. No change to the equation is needed,
  provided the Table IV-VIII values are replaced by the new seeded runs (task 6).
